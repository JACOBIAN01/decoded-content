# Decoded #12 research: RBI draft stop switch and trusted person. Built Thu 26 Nov 2026 for Fri 27 Nov

## Documented
Draft "Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Responsible Business Conduct) Fifth Amendment Directions, 2026".
- Released 23 Nov (Business Standard). Draft, not final.
- Applies to commercial banks other than Small Finance Banks, Payments Banks, Regional Rural Banks and Local Area Banks (TaxGuru verbatim preamble; MediaNama).
- Effective 1 July 2027, or earlier if a bank decides; parts of the negligence definition from 1 Jan 2027 (TaxGuru verbatim; Business Standard).
- Stop switch: "a facility through which a customer can disable all customer initiated future debits" (TaxGuru verbatim). Blocks customer-initiated debits, new mandates/standing instructions, UPI registration on new third-party apps; incoming money, existing recurring mandates and bank-initiated dues continue (Business Standard). Re-enabling needs authentication; banks may add a cooling period (Business Standard; TaxGuru summary).
- Trusted person: voluntary, 18+ (TaxGuru verbatim definition), customer sets the threshold, one hour to approve, no response or rejection means declined, 24-hour cooling period to set up; excludes UPI, merchant and recurring payments; no view of balances (Business Standard; MediaNama for exclusions).
- Digital payment channels disabled by default for new accounts until consent and authentication (Business Standard; MediaNama). Customer-set limits per transaction and per day (TaxGuru verbatim; MediaNama).
- 1930 is the national cyber financial fraud helpline (search results incl. PIB and Kerala Police pages; pages not opened).
