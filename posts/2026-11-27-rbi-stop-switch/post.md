## Caption
RBI wants banks to give you a stop switch for new outgoing payments. How would it work?

It's a draft released on 23 November, not a rule yet, for commercial banks, proposed from 1 July 2027. The switch would block new payments you start, while money coming in and existing auto-payments continue. An optional "trusted person" could approve big transfers (not UPI).
