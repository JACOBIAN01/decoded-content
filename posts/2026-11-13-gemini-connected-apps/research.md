# Decoded #08 research: Gemini Connected Apps and human review (built Thu 12 Nov 2026 for Fri 13 Nov)

## Documented (Google, as quoted verbatim in the 12 Nov trend scan, which opened both pages that day)
- Google Gemini Help, "About personalization with Connected Apps" (support.google.com/gemini/answer/16836988; page undated): "Data from Connected Apps is used and human reviewed per the Gemini Apps Privacy Notice"; "a subset of Connected Apps data processed by Gemini is reviewed by human reviewers (including trained service providers)"; "Please don't connect your apps to Gemini Apps if they contain confidential information"; "Gemini does not train generative AI models directly on your Gmail inbox, Drive…" but "we train our generative AI models off of these summaries, excerpts, generated media, and inferences" (training applies with Keep Activity on).
- Gemini Apps Privacy Hub (support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961): reviewed chats are kept "up to three years", disconnected from the account, and not deleted when you delete your activity; disconnecting an app "doesn't delete any data from Gemini Apps Activity"; with Keep Activity off, future chats are not used to improve Google AI, but Google says it still uses chats "to help protect Google, our users, and the public, including with help from human reviewers".
- Independent coverage: AI Weekly (9-10 Nov), Inc (10 Nov), HN front page (9 Nov, 733 pts). Used only to show the story is live, not as the source of any claim on the slides.
