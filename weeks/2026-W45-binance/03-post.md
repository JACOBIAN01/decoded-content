## Caption
About $2.8 billion of the crypto stolen from Binance on 29 October was frozen, according to Elliptic. The Bitcoin and Ether couldn't be.

Why does some money have an off switch?

One layer deeper, in 9 slides: stablecoins like USDT and USDC are issued by companies, and their contracts keep a blocklist. Bitcoin and Ether have no issuer, so the network only checks one thing: is the signature valid? That's why Tether and Circle could stop part of the theft, and why the FBI is asking companies to refuse the thieves' addresses instead.

A freeze protected victims this time. It's also one company deciding whether money can move.

Should one company be able to freeze anyone's money? Yes or no, and why?
